# The team's working files

The working files of the nine agents that honed this design on 20 November 2026. They are a
record, not part of the design: the design is the four files one folder up. Paths inside
these files point at the folder the team worked in, which no longer exists.

## The brief

- `team-brief.md`: the rules every agent worked to, with the settled list word for word.

## The reports, in the order they were written

Ground:

- `01-analyst-walkthrough.md`: a pricing analyst walks through the design as it was, on
  eight scenarios, and ranks the 15 problems that matter most.
- `02-state-of-the-art.md`: how other tools and the literature handle the same job, as 37
  pattern cards with their sources.
- `03-codebase.md`: what Haute's code and its model libraries can support, every claim with
  a file path.

Design:

- `04-workflow-draft.md`, `05-analytics-draft.md`, `06-ui-draft.md`: the three designers'
  drafts. They are longer than the final files and hold ideas that were later cut.

Challenge:

- `07-first-time-walkthrough.md`: a first-time analyst tries seven tasks from the drafts
  alone, and scores each.
- `08-red-team.md`: what was wrong with the drafts: conflicts with settled lines,
  statistics that would mislead, claims the code does not bear out, and what to cut.

## The two folders

- `before/`: `overview.md`, `workflow.md` and `ui.md` as they stood before the team's work.
  `workflow.md` and `ui.md` were replaced whole, so this is the only copy of the earlier
  versions.
- `final/`: the editor's files as delivered. The live `workflow.md`, `ui.md` and
  `analytics.md` are these with paths tidied and a few wordings fixed.
  `final/overview-changes.md` is the fullest record of the editor's decisions: every new
  proposal, every change to proposals 1 to 19, the findings it rebutted and why, and the
  findings it resolved and where.
